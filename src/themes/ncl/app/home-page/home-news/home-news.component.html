CSIR-NCL Digital Repository

An institutional repository for the collection, preservation and dissemination of the intellectual output of CSIR-NCL, Pune.

The repository contains a growing collection of research articles, conference papers, patents, theses, dissertations, technical reports, and other scholarly works produced by CSIR-NCL researchers. It also includes datasets, software, and other research outputs that support the scientific and technological activities of the institute.

The repository is open to all users and provides free access to the deposited materials. It is a valuable resource for researchers, students, and anyone interested in the scientific and technological activities of CSIR-NCL, Pune.
